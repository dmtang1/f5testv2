Privacy

This prototype collects as little as possible.

Results stay on your device

Your answers and Fiveprint are stored in this browser. Share links encode the type and scores in the URL. They do not include your email.

Email

If you opt in, we store your email, optional name, type, axis scores, the time, where the form was submitted, and a record that you consented. We don’t ask for anything else.

Analytics

Optional events cover things like starting the test and opening a playbook cell. They do not include your name or email. Nothing is sent before you choose Allow analytics.

Deletion

To ask for an email to be deleted, use the contact on the privacy policy linked below.
